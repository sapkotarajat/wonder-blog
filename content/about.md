## About Wonder Archive

Wonder Archive tells the strange side of history: absurd laws, brutal punishments, rulers' bizarre demands, and
inventions that were centuries ahead of their time.

Every article here is the long version of a short video on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@WonderArchive-p2b).

The articles and videos are produced with the help of AI and an automated pipeline. We aim for accuracy, but history
is full of disputed details — if you spot a mistake, let us know through the channel.
